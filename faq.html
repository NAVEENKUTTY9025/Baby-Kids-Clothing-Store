GOT QUESTIONS?

We've got answers

Everything you need to know about shopping with LITTLE NEST. Find answers to our most asked questions below.

All Shopping Products Shipping Returns Sustainability Sizing & Care

Ordering from LITTLE NEST is easy! Simply browse our collection, select the items you love, choose the size and quantity, and add them to your cart. When you're ready, proceed to checkout, enter your shipping and payment details, and confirm your order. You'll receive a confirmation email right away.

We process orders quickly, but if you need to make changes, please contact us within 2 hours of placing your order. We'll do our best to accommodate your request. Once an order has been shipped, we won't be able to make changes.

We offer sizes from Newborn to 6T (toddler). We also carry pre-walker shoes and accessories. Each product page includes a detailed size guide to help you find the perfect fit.

Yes! Our cotton is GOTS-certified organic, and we use eco-friendly, non-toxic dyes. All our products are tested for harmful substances and are safe for babies' sensitive skin.

We offer free standard shipping on all orders over $75. For orders under $75, shipping starts at $5.99. Expedited shipping options are also available at checkout.

Currently, we ship within the United States and Canada. We're working on expanding to more countries soon. Stay tuned!

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in their original packaging with tags attached. Return shipping is free. Simply contact our support team to initiate a return.

To start a return, email us at hello@littlenest.com with your order number and the items you'd like to return. We'll send you a prepaid shipping label and return instructions. It's that simple!

We're committed to sustainability in every aspect of our business:

  • Organic materials: GOTS-certified cotton and eco-friendly dyes.
  • Ethical production: Fair labor practices and safe working conditions.
  • Minimal waste: We use recycled packaging and reduce plastic wherever possible.
  • Timeless designs: We create pieces that last, so you can buy less and choose well.

All our packaging is recyclable and made from recycled materials. We use tissue paper, boxes, and mailers that are 100% curbside recyclable. We also offer the option to include a gift note on recycled paper.

LITTLE NEST clothes run true to size, but since babies grow quickly, we recommend sizing up if your little one is in between sizes. Each product page has a detailed size guide with weight and height ranges to help you choose.

To keep our organic cotton super soft and durable, we recommend machine washing in cold water on a gentle cycle with a mild, baby-friendly detergent. Tumble dry on low heat or hang dry to minimize natural shrinkage.

Our Care Guide

Simple guidelines to keep your LITTLE NEST pieces looking like new for generations.

Baby-Safe Soap

Use gentle, eco-friendly detergents free from harsh chemicals, optical brighteners, and synthetic fragrances to protect sensitive skin.

Cold Wash Always

Machine wash on cold, gentle cycle. Cold water prevents shrinkage, preserves the delicate organic cotton fibers, and saves energy.

Tumble Dry Low

Tumble dry on low heat or hang dry. High heat can damage organic threads and lead to natural shrinkage of the cotton fabric.
